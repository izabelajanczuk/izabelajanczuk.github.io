About

I design at the intersection of place, material, and community — always starting from research, always arriving somewhere sensory.

Note

I'm Iza — a multidisciplinary designer currently studying Environmental Design and Sustainability at the University of Washington, after three years at IE University in Madrid. My practice spans UX, industrial design, and branding, with a thread of sustainability running through all of it.

I'm drawn to the edges where disciplines meet: where a bus stop becomes a question about who gets to inhabit public space, or where an app becomes a lens onto a global ecosystem. The best problems, for me, are the ones that resist a single medium.

Alongside studio work I've taken courses in product development, strategy, and entrepreneurship, and gained experience through non-profit and museum roles — spaces that care about community in ways that complicate easy design solutions.

Outside of work, I'm a photographer. I shoot mostly on film, mostly looking for light in quiet, unremarkable places. I grew up in Poland, live in Seattle, and travel when I can.
